# Canadian BEV Driver Demographics Summary

This document summarizes the demographic profile of Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV) drivers in Canada, based on the CAA Canadian EV Driver Study. All data pertaining to PHEV (Plug-in Hybrid) drivers has been excluded as requested.

## 1. Age Distribution
Canadian BEV drivers skew older, with an average age of **51.3 years**. Over 50% of BEV drivers are 55 years of age or older.

| Age Group | Percentage (BEV) |
| :--- | :--- |
| 18-34 | 8% |
| 35-44 | 19% |
| 45-54 | 24% |
| 55-64 | 25% |
| 65-74 | 19% |
| 75+ | 6% |

## 2. Gender
The BEV ownership demographic heavily skews toward males, with females making up less than a quarter of the drivers.

| Gender | Percentage (BEV) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Male | 74% |
| Female | 23% |
| Other | 3% |

## 3. Marital Status
Consistent with the older age profile, the vast majority of BEV drivers are married or in a domestic partnership.

| Marital Status | Percentage (BEV) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Married / Domestic Partnership | 83% |
| Single | 8% |
| Divorced | 4% |
| Widowed | 2% |
| Other | 1% |

## 4. Housing & Home Ownership
Homeownership is a significant factor in BEV adoption, largely due to the ease of installing home charging stations compared to renting or living in multi-unit dwellings.

- **Home Ownership**: 
  - Own Home: **88%**
  - Rent Home: 11%
- **Dwelling Type**:
  - Single Family Dwelling: **77%**
  - Multi-Unit Dwelling: 18%

*Data Source: PlugShare Research / CAA Canadian EV Driver Study (2022-2023)*
